Pentomino Calendar Solver

About

This is a web-based solver for the puzzle Calendarium by Dmitry Andreev (Pluton). The original version was in Russian, while english variants are available from sources like Puzzle Guy.

The goal of the puzzle is to arrange 10 pentomino tiles in such a way that they cover all squares except for today's month, day, and weekday. There is one problem with this: finding a solution is incredibly hard. And, you know, some mornings I just to complete a calendar before my coffee is finished.
